About N8 Detailz

I’m Nathan, the owner of N8 Detailz. I started detailing out of my mom’s cars. Since then, I’ve personally detailed hundreds of vehicles across LA and built out my own van to bring professional detailing to homes and workplaces across Los Angeles and Orange County.

I carry my own spot-free water and run my detailing equipment on a power bank to keep noise down. You don’t need to provide anything.
